The Format

What Is British Parliamentary Debate?

4
Teams in every round
8
Speakers total (2 per team)
7
Minutes per speech
1
Motion debated by all

BP is the world's most widely used competitive debate format. Two teams argue for the motion (Government), two argue against (Opposition). All four teams compete to be ranked 1st–4th in the same round — cooperation within a side, competition between all four.

Parliament

The Four Teams

Opening Bench
OG
Opening Government

Prime Minister & Deputy PM

Defines the motion and sets the model. Makes the strongest affirmative case. Must be first to speak.

OO
Opening Opposition

Leader of Opp & Deputy LO

Challenges OG's definition and case. Establishes the opposition's counter-model or refutation.

Closing Bench
CG
Closing Government

Member for Gov & Gov Whip

Must bring an Extension — a new gov argument. Whip summarises and rebuts (no new arguments).

CO
Closing Opposition

Member for Opp & Opp Whip

Must bring an Extension — a new opp argument. Whip summarises and rebuts (no new arguments).

Interruptions

Points of Information (POIs)

POI Window Open (mins 1–6)
0 min 1 min 2 min 3 min 4 min 5 min 6 min 7 min
✋

Offering a POI

Stand up, say "Point of information" or "On that point." Sit immediately if declined — never persist.

🎯

Accepting a POI

Say "Yes" or "I'll take that." The POI must be brief — under 15 seconds. Aim to accept 1–2 per speech.

🛡️

Declining a POI

Say "Not now" or gesture to sit them down. Declining too many signals weakness to the judges.

⚖️

POI Strategy

Quality beats quantity. One sharp POI that exposes a gap beats five weak ones that get declined.

Adjudication

What Judges Look For

💡
Content
Quality of arguments, evidence, and logical reasoning. Is the case compelling and well-structured?
🎤
Style
Clarity, pace, confidence, and persuasiveness of delivery. How engaging and credible is the speaker?
🗺️
Strategy
Role fulfilment, rebuttal quality, and understanding of the debate's structure. Does the speaker help their team win?
