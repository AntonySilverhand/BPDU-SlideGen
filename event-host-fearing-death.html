Critical Rule

Closing Teams Must
Extend, Not Repeat

Must Be New

Closing teams introduce distinct arguments the opening bench did not make.

Must Add Value

Extensions deepen the debate. They are not mere repetition with different words.

Logistics

Prep Time: 15 Minutes

You have a combined 15 minutes to prepare. Use this time wisely: brief your team, refine arguments, anticipate rebuttals, and organize your speeches.

Prep time is shared across all four teams. Speakers will be called when ready.

You're Ready

Make Your Case

Speak with conviction. Listen with care. Engage with your opponents' best arguments, not their weakest. Show the judges why your vision is superior.
